A LITTLE CONTEXT

More than a list of tools.

I’m from Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, and currently pursuing an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at NIT Rourkela.

I’m drawn to the process of figuring things out: understanding a problem, trying an approach, learning from what breaks, and making the next version better. My projects have taken me from predictive maintenance and scheduling systems to privacy focused health data and document question answering.

I try to stay grounded about what I know. There’s always more to learn, and I’m comfortable starting with a question rather than pretending to have every answer.
